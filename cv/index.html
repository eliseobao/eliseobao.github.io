Research Interests

  • NLP for Mental Health — detecting and explaining depression-related symptoms in social media using clinical frameworks such as the DSM-5 and BDI-II.
  • Explainable AI — developing interpretable methods and clinically grounded explanations for mental-health assessment.
  • Conversational AI — building conversational agents that support clinicians in reviewing and assessing mental-health information.
  • Persona Simulation and Evaluation — evaluating how faithfully LLM-based personas simulate real users for mental-health research.
  • Multilingual NLP — adapting language models to underrepresented languages, including Galician.

Publications

  • 2026
    Eval4Sim: An Evaluation Framework for Persona Simulation
    Proc. of the 35th ACM Int'l Conference on Information and Knowledge Management (CIKM 2026), Rome, Italy. Forthcoming

    Introduces a three-dimensional framework (adherence, consistency, naturalness) for evaluating how well LLM personas simulate human conversation.

  • 2026
    Improving clinical reliability of LLM reasoning for depression assessment via structured generation and GRPO
    Science Progress, 109(3)

    Shows GRPO-trained LLMs can explain depression-screening decisions through DSM-5-aligned reasoning without sacrificing classification accuracy.

  • 2026
    Learning Evidence of Depression Symptoms via Prompt Induction
    Proc. of the 49th International ACM SIGIR Conference (SIGIR 2026), Melbourne, Australia

    Proposes Symptom Induction, compressing labeled examples into interpretable guidelines that improve LLM classification of depressive symptoms.

  • 2025
    How does depression talk on social media? Modeling depression language with relevance-based statistical language models
    Online Social Networks and Media, 50, 100339

    Builds relevance-based statistical language models of depression-related language to rank users by risk across Reddit and Twitter.

  • 2025
    ReDSM5: A Reddit Dataset for DSM-5 Depression Detection
    Proc. of the 34th ACM Int'l Conference on Information and Knowledge Management (CIKM 2025), Seoul, Republic of Korea

    A 1,484-post Reddit dataset with sentence-level, clinician-annotated DSM-5 depressive-symptom labels and rationales.

  • 2025
    Towards Explainable and Safe Systems for Health Data
    SIGIR 2025 Doctoral Consortium, Padua, Italy

    Doctoral consortium paper outlining a research plan for transparent, explainable, and safe health information systems.

  • 2025
    MindWell: A Conversational Agent for Professional Depression Screening on Social Media
    Advances in Information Retrieval — ECIR 2025, LNCS, Part V, pp. 47-52, Lucca, Italy

    Demonstrates an open-source RAG chatbot that helps clinicians review social media history for BDI-II depressive symptoms.

  • 2024
    Explainable depression symptom detection in social media
    Health Information Science and Systems, 12(1), 47

    Transformer-based models that both detect and explain depressive symptom markers in social media text.

  • 2024
    Adapting Large Language Models for Underrepresented Languages
    VII Congreso XoveTIC, Universidade da Coruña — CICAS-Sngular Award

    Introduces a Galician-adapted Llama 3.1 model and instruction dataset, outperforming the base model and another Galician model.

  • 2024
    MindWell: an open-source chatbot to assist in the detection and monitoring of depressive disorders
    M.Sc. Thesis, Universidade da Coruña

    Designed and evaluated the MindWell chatbot with a professional psychologist.

  • 2022
    Ranking of Reddit users using Relevance Models for depressive disorders
    B.Sc. Thesis, Universidade da Coruña

    Built depression-risk vocabularies and ranking methods from Reddit using relevance-based language models.
